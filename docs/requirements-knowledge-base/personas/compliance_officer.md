# Compliance Officer

## Role

A user responsible for reviewing compliance, ownership, governance, and regulatory findings for a company or industry.

## Goal

Obtain a structured compliance report that consolidates ownership analysis, governance insights, regulatory context, and supporting evidence retrieved from trusted external sources through the platform's MCP-powered research pipeline.

## Primary Workflows

* Research Request Processing
* Multi-Agent Execution

## Success Criteria

* Compliance findings are accurate and well-structured.
* Ownership and governance insights are supported by trusted external sources.
* Regulatory context is included when relevant.
* Reports provide verifiable supporting evidence for compliance-related findings.

## Out of Scope

* Market data ingestion.
* Knowledge synchronization.
* Infrastructure benchmarking.
* Internal workflow administration or approval management.
