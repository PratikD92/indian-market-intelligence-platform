# Casual Investor

## Role

A user seeking AI-assisted investment research without performing extensive manual analysis.

## Goal

Obtain a reliable research response generated from trusted market data, company filings, and AI-powered analysis through a single research request.

## Primary Workflows

* Research Request Processing
* Multi-Agent Execution

## Success Criteria

* Research requests are processed successfully.
* Responses are grounded in trusted data sources.
* Source citations are included in the generated research.
* Responses are delivered within the platform's performance targets.

## Out of Scope

* Advanced financial modeling.
* Compliance review workflows.
* Portfolio management or trading execution.
