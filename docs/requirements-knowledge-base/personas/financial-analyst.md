# Financial Analyst

## Role

A user requiring comprehensive financial and industry analysis supported by coordinated AI agents and structured research outputs.

## Goal

Generate detailed research by combining financial statement analysis, industry context, external information retrieval, and synthesized AI reasoning.

## Primary Workflows

* Research Request Processing
* Multi-Agent Execution
* Report Generation & Approval

## Success Criteria

* Financial analysis is comprehensive and well-structured.
* Multiple AI agents contribute specialized insights.
* Reports are generated successfully after workflow completion.
* Analysis remains grounded in retrieved sources.

## Out of Scope

* Compliance ownership review.
* Infrastructure benchmarking.
* Manual workflow management outside the defined approval process.
* Real-time trading or investment execution.
