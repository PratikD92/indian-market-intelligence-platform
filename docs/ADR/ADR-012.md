# ADR-012: Separate Market Data Ingestion from Knowledge Synchronization

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-22

## Context

The platform ingests heterogeneous financial data (PDFs, HTML, CSV, Excel, JSON, filings, and transcripts) before converting it into semantic knowledge for AI retrieval. Initially, parsing and chunking responsibilities overlapped between ingestion and knowledge synchronization, creating unclear ownership.

## Decision

Separate the pipeline into two independent microservices with distinct responsibilities.

- **Market Data Service** owns fetching, parsing, validation, normalization, metadata generation, writing structured records to PostgreSQL and firing `DataIngested` event.
- **Knowledge Sync Service** consumes `DataIngested` event, reads normalized records, applies content-type-driven chunking, generates embeddings, extracts relationships, updates GraphRAG and pgvector, and invalidates cache after a successful update.

## Consequences

### Advantages

- Clear separation between operational and semantic processing.
- PostgreSQL remains the single source of truth.
- Knowledge synchronization becomes independently scalable.
- Easier maintenance and testing of each pipeline stage.

### Trade-offs

- Requires asynchronous coordination through Pub/Sub.
- Additional synchronization step before semantic indexes are updated.
- More services increase operational complexity.

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Reason Rejected |
|------------|-----------------|
| Single service handling both ingestion and semantic enrichment | Rejected due to overlapping responsibilities, tighter coupling, and reduced scalability. |
