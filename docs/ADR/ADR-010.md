# ADR-010: Use GraphRAG for Relationship-Aware Retrieval

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-21

## Context

The platform answers financial research queries that depend on relationships between companies, directors, ownership structures, filings, and industry entities. Semantic similarity alone cannot reliably traverse these connections or provide relationship-aware reasoning.

## Decision

Use **GraphRAG** to complement semantic retrieval by incorporating entity relationships into the retrieval pipeline.

- Knowledge Sync Service extracts entities and relationships from normalized content.
- GraphRAG builds and queries the relationship graph.
- Semantic retrieval (pgvector) identifies relevant content, while GraphRAG expands retrieval through connected entities before response generation.
- The Research / Agent Service uses GraphRAG for ownership analysis, director relationships, and compliance-focused queries.

## Consequences

### Advantages

- Improves multi-hop and relationship-aware retrieval.
- Provides richer context for financial and compliance queries.
- Enhances explainability by grounding responses in connected entities.
- Complements semantic search rather than replacing it.

### Trade-offs

- Additional processing during knowledge synchronization.
- Relationship extraction requires ongoing maintenance and validation.
- Increases retrieval pipeline complexity.

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Reason Rejected |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Neo4j Graph Database** | Rejected to avoid maintaining a separate graph database, reduce infrastructure and operational cost, and keep relationship-aware retrieval integrated within the GraphRAG pipeline. |
