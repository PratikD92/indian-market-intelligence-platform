# ADR-018: Centralize AI Quality Assurance in the LLM Evaluation Service

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-09-09

## Context

The platform distinguishes between offline evaluation (pre-release quality assurance) and online evaluation (production AI observability) to avoid mixing regression testing with live system monitoring.

## Decision

Centralize offline AI evaluation into a dedicated LLM Evaluation Service, while keeping online AI observability as a separate platform capability through Arize Phoenix.

- **Offline (LLM Evaluation Service)**: Owns the Golden Dataset and executes Ragas, DeepEval, and Promptfoo during CI/CD and manual regression runs.

- **Online (Observablility Stack - Platform Capability)**: Arize Phoenix continuously captures production traces, token usage, latency, drift, and inference costs.

- **MLflow**: Stores experiment metadata and evaluation results generated by the LLM Evaluation Service.

- The LLM Evaluation Service is deployed as a platform microservice alongside other cross-cutting platform services (OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana, Arize Phoenix, and MLflow), rather than as part of any business workflow.

## Consequences

### Advantages

- Centralized AI quality gates across the platform.
- Consistent evaluation using shared Golden Datasets.
- Prevents duplicated evaluation logic across services.
- Supports automated regression testing before deployment.
- Clear separation between offline quality assurance and online production monitoring, reducing operational coupling and making the AI lifecycle easier to manage.

### Trade-offs

- Additional service to maintain.
- Requires ongoing curation of Golden Datasets.
- CI/CD execution time increases as evaluations grow.

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Reason Rejected |
|------------|-----------------|
| Embedding evaluation logic inside each microservice | Rejected because it duplicates tooling, creates inconsistent evaluation standards, and makes CI/CD quality gates harder to maintain. |
